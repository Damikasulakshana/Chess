# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain PHP with HTML/CSS — no framework. Static landing page served via PHP.

## Users

Students and faculty members of the University of Ruhuna, Faculty of Technology, who want to learn about and participate in the faculty's chess tournament. Visitors are prospective participants checking event details, and spectators following the event.

## Product Purpose

A landing page for the University of Ruhuna, Faculty of Technology Chess Tournament — a one-time competitive event. The page communicates the event's purpose, schedule, and identity, and invites eligible participants to engage. Success means the page clearly conveys the tournament's prestige and information at a glance, and feels worthy of a university-level competitive event.

## Positioning

The official digital presence for this specific tournament. It is the single authoritative source for event details, differentiated by its connection to the University of Ruhuna Faculty of Technology and by design craft that elevates a university event beyond typical student-org pages.

## Operating Context

A standalone landing page — no multi-page app, no database, no user accounts. Visitors arrive via shared links (WhatsApp groups, faculty notice boards, social media). Viewed primarily on mobile phones by Sri Lankan university students, with some desktop access.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single landing page (no routing, no backend logic beyond PHP includes)
- Event information: date, venue, rules, contact
- No registration form or payment flow at this stage (landing page only)
- Must load quickly on mobile connections common in Sri Lanka

## Brand Commitments

- University of Ruhuna, Faculty of Technology — use official university colors where identifiable
- No existing logo or brand assets provided; the design must feel institutional and credible without them

## Evidence on Hand

- No logos, photos, testimonials, or event history provided
- Future work must not fabricate testimonials, past results, or participant quotes

## Product Principles

1. **Institutional credibility** — the page must feel official, not hobbyist
2. **Clarity above all** — event facts (when, where, who, what) must be instantly scannable
3. **Mobile-first reach** — most visitors will be students on phones
4. **Celebration of the game** — chess as an intellectual sport deserves a design that honors its character

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard was established. Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA as a reasonable baseline for a university event page.
